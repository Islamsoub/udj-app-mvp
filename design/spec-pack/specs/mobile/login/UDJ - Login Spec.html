UDJ — Login Spec

Implementation reference for the polished Login / Connexion screen. This pass keeps the clean, flat reference design and adds exactly two upgrades: a deeper jade hero (160° gradient, jade→jade700) and a white form sheet with rounded top corners that lifts over the hero. Everything else stays close to the original — a bare white logo (no badge), simple labels, two-style inputs, a pill CTA. Six states share one scaffold: default · submitting · error · locked-out · session-expired · skeleton. Supports light, dark and RTL (Arabic). This is a visual polish pass; the code-level token / keyboard items from the audit are noted in §8 but out of scope here.

0 Key decisions

Decision 1 · The hero

Keep the flat hero — just make the green deeper and let the sheet round over it.

The hero stays clean and flat like the reference: a bare white U mark (U only — the mortarboard is retired everywhere as of 27 Oct 2026), bold white brand name, and tagline on default. The one change is colour — a 160° jade → jade700 gradient instead of the flat block, giving a richer, deeper feel. Three soft white blobs sit behind for depth. Brand name forces white-space:nowrap so "Université de Djibouti" never wraps. No fixed height — the hero sizes to content.

Deeper gradient · bare logo · no badge · intrinsic height.
Decision 2 · Form surface

The form is a white sheet that lifts 28px over the hero.

The body becomes a surface card with rSheet 30 top corners, marginTop -28 and a soft upward shadow — the same "sheet over hero" depth used by the QR and Session sheets. This gives the screen a clear figure/ground and a premium first impression instead of input fields floating on bg.

surface · rTop 30 · overlap -28 · upward shadow.
Decision 3 · Input styles

Two-style inputs, matching the reference: outlined ID, filled password.

The reference uses two deliberately different field looks and we keep them: the student-ID field is surface + hair2 hairline (a clean outlined box, mono value), and the password field is a soft jadeFaint fill with no border. Both are 58 tall, r16. Focus adds a jade border + 3px jadeFaint ring; error tints both dangerBg + danger border; disabled goes sunken.

ID outlined · password filled · jade focus ring · dangerBg error.
Decision 4 · State preview

A minimal frosted pill bar pins to the bottom of the screen.

The dev-only state switcher is a slim frosted bar (surface@92% + blur) with a scrollable row of pills — active pill jade, the rest grey. No label, no heavy chrome, matching the reference's simple switcher. In the live prototype the same six states are also drivable from the control rail.

Frosted blur bar · scroll pills · jade active.

One scroll, no nested regions. The hero scrolls with the form (tall states like locked-out simply scroll). The demo bar is the only fixed element — there is no floating nav on the pre-auth screen. The form sheet flex:1 so its white fills to the bottom on short states.

7 Interaction & motion

  • Field focus — border → jade, 4px jadeFaint ring, 0.15s ease.
  • CTA submitting — label swaps to a 22px spinner (udjSpin .8s linear); button stays jade, fields disable.
  • Skeleton — bones pulse udjBone (opacity .5→.85, 1s ease-in-out); hero badge + 2 lines, form 2 label/field pairs + button.
  • Demo bar — frosted (surface@86% + blur 12px), horizontal scroll, active pill jade with 0 2 8 jade@25% shadow.
  • Forgot / help / change — wired as real <button> affordances (no dead Pressables in the prototype).
