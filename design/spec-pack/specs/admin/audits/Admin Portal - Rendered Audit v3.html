Rendered Audit v3

Third audit pass, conducted against rendered output rather than source. Twenty-one localhost captures of the deployed admin portal, compared frame by frame against the signed-off Implementation Spec (§10–23) and the prototype in admin/. Thirteen screens plus one cross-cutting section.

Date · 7 Aug 2026

Findings · 62
Why this pass exists. Audits v1 and v2 were conducted source-to-source — prototype JSX diffed against branch CSS and components. That method finds numeric mismatches and it did. It structurally cannot find defects that only exist at paint time: a font that is declared correctly but never loads, a browser-applied autofill fill, a gradient that is present in source but clipped by an ancestor, a container width inherited from further up the tree than the files read. Every finding in §0 and roughly half of everything below was invisible to both earlier passes. Items already logged in v1/v2 and still unfixed are marked Repeat.
Capture-scale note. The batch was captured at a device pixel ratio of 1.25 (CSS viewport ≈ 1536px, not 1920). Every measurement in this document has been divided by 1.25 before comparison — raw pixel deltas taken straight off these images will read 25% high. One capture (Modifier la séance) includes browser chrome and was taken at 1.0; it is excluded from measurement. Shell geometry normalises cleanly: sidebar 248px ✓, top bar 64px ✓ — both exactly to spec.

Bug The typeface never loads. The entire portal renders in a serif fallback.
SpecPlus Jakarta Sans (A_SANS) for all UI text; DM Mono (A_MONO) for identifiers, eyebrows, timestamps and numeric labels. BuildEvery string on every one of the 21 captures renders in a Times-class serif — headings, nav labels, table headers, matricules, schedule times, the "ADMIN · UDJ" eyebrow, the footer. Neither family is reaching the browser. ImpactThis is the single largest visual defect in the build and it is invisible in source, where the family names are declared correctly. It also erases a whole semantic layer: the spec uses mono deliberately to mark machine data — UDJ-2024-1298, 08:00–09:30, BBA101, role names, section eyebrows. With one serif for everything, identifiers and prose are typographically identical. Fix this first; it will change how every other screen reads and may resolve several spacing findings below on its own.

Fix in this order

  • 1 · The webfont. One root cause, 21 screens. Everything else should be judged after it lands.
  • 2 · Dashboard aggregation. The 0.0/20 "Insuffisant" tile and the empty distribution chart are the same failure, and it is the first thing every user sees.
  • 3 · The four scope contradictions. Académique's pills vs the context bar; the schedule create modal ignoring scope; the missing context bar on the dashboard; the three empty-state strings. All one architectural seam.
  • 4 · Misleading states. Green checks on zero-session subjects; the amber empty-queue tab; the primary-weight "Définir comme courant"; the disabled-looking "Générer"; the destructive "Supprimer" in the reader modal.
  • 5 · The size sweep. Icon tiles (+25%), GradeCell height, student row pitch, news thumbnails, login form width. One pass through the shared primitives.
  • 6 · Copy. Plurals, the three domains, "Youth", the truncated placeholder and labels.

Still unverified

  • Grade-entry sticky save bar — needs a capture with a dirty cell and the page scrolled. The v2 z-order finding is neither confirmed nor cleared.
  • Six screens never captured — Paramètres, Administrateurs, Journal d'audit, Import CSV wizard, Faire l'appel (session marking), Notifications composer. Unread at source in v2 and unseen at render now.
  • Interaction states — hover, focus, open dropdowns, toasts, the advanced-filters panel, modal confirmations. Nothing in this batch shows a transient state.
  • Other personas — everything here is REGISTRAR. SUPER_ADMIN, FACULTY_ADMIN and NEWS_EDITOR views are unaudited.
